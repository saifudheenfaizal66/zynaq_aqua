Production Hardware Breakdown

Physical Design Specifications

Every contour of the FinFloat housing was custom engineered for hydrodynamic balance, anti-corrosion marine endurance, and solar autonomy.

Hydrodynamic Twin-Pontoon Chassis

1350 × 650 mm

Catamaran geometry (1350 mm in length × 650 mm in width) ensures zero rollover in surface chop and high-velocity aerator currents. Inscribed with “Engineered for the Water” and “Powered by Precision”.

Bow AI Optical & Acoustic Vision

AI Edge

Dual recessed bow apertures house LiDAR sensors and acoustic hydrophones (cameras are not utilized in this module) that continuously score biomass surface appetite to eliminate unconsumed feed waste.

High-Gain Telemetry & Strobe Tower

4G LTE

High-gain cellular antenna streams real-time data packets via 4G LTE to the standard cloud gateway, paired with an emergency color-coded status beacon for night navigation.

Integrated Solar & Passive Cooling Deck

72-Hr Battery

Upper deck integrates weatherproof photovoltaic cells with micro-louvered convection heat vents, keeping internal battery and motor controllers cool in Kerala's tropical sun.

Gravity-Assisted Precision Dispenser

Anti-Clog Monitored

Low-friction gravity dispersing system with active anti-clog feed throat monitoring, ensuring uniform pellet release and zero jamming in tropical humidity.

Intelligent Convergence

The Smart Aquaculture Ecosystem

FinFloat is just the beginning. We are uniting hardware, automation, sensors, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and cloud software to create an autonomous farming ecosystem.

PHASE 01

Sensors & Hardware

FinFloat deployed directly on the pond water surface. Submerged optical probes continuously sample Dissolved Oxygen, Temperature, pH, and Salinity.

PHASE 02

Edge Telemetry

Onboard microcontrollers process sensor signals at the edge and securely stream live data packets over high-speed 4G LTE to the farm cloud gateway.

PHASE 03

AI & Analytics

Intelligent algorithms analyze feeding behavior, diurnal oxygen curves, and ambient conditions to formulate the exact micro-feeding schedule needed.

PHASE 04

Autonomous Action

FinFloat dispenses precision rations automatically while farmers monitor pond health, aeration status, and biomass gains from anywhere via mobile app.
