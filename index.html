What FinFloat Does

Operational Pipeline

How It Works

Three autonomous stages eliminate manual farm intervention — from submerged sensing to precision actuation.

Stage 01

Submerged Multi-Sensing

Continuous probe telemetry at critical pond depths — DO, temperature, pH, and turbidity sampled in real time, 24/7.

Stage 02

Edge Anomaly Evaluation

Cloud analytics log every shift to build a live operational farm twin — detecting hypoxia windows before they become critical.

Stage 03

Automated Actuation

Optimizes FCR by releasing feed strictly during peak oxygen windows — reducing waste, preventing ammonia spikes, maximizing biomass gain.
